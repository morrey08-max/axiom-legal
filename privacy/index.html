Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Axiom handles information when you use our public website, submit a commercial inquiry, use Axiom tools, or connect an external service such as LinkedIn.

Effective 26 September 2026Public website + authorized integrations
Plain-language summaryWe collect only information needed to operate Axiom, respond to requests, measure whether our services work, and run features you explicitly authorize. Axiom does not sell personal information. External-account access is limited to permissions granted by the account holder and capabilities actually made available by the provider.

1. Who “Axiom” means

In this policy, “Axiom” means the operator of the Axiom service and brand. Using the name “Axiom” in this policy does not by itself assert that a separately incorporated legal entity exists. Where a specific incorporated entity or contracting party is relevant to an engagement, it will be identified in the applicable official record, proposal, invoice, statement of work or contract, and that document will govern the matters it covers.

2. Scope

This policy applies to Axiom's public web properties, commercial-intelligence and automation workflows, inquiry forms, first-party product measurement, and supported account integrations. Separate Axiom products may publish additional notices where their data flows differ.

3. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Inquiry and contact information: name, work email, company or organization, business goal, geography, timing, and the message or context you submit.
  • Business content: product, catalogue, opportunity, supplier, buyer, market, tender, partnership or other commercial information you ask Axiom to research or analyze.
  • Files or data you intentionally provide: only where a feature allows you to submit them.

Limited first-party usage information

Our public site records a limited set of first-party product events used to understand whether the service is functioning and which workflows are used. Current event metadata is intentionally narrow and may include fields such as source, selected business intent, viewport class or workflow state. The public Axiom site does not currently use advertising trackers.

Catalogue-audit input

When you use the public catalogue checker, submitted catalogue text is processed to generate the requested audit. The current public-site implementation records a limited completion event but does not intentionally place the full catalogue text in the site's analytics event record.

Asset-related inquiries

If your inquiry concerns asset sourcing or asset disposal, the inquiry may be routed from the public website to Axiom's internal opportunity-intake infrastructure so it can be reviewed, qualified and followed up.

4. Connected services and LinkedIn

If you choose to connect LinkedIn or another external service, Axiom processes only data made available under the scopes you authorize and the provider actually permits. Depending on the feature and permission set, this may include profile identity, posts, scheduled content, comments, reactions, engagement metrics and related identifiers.

  • Axiom does not claim access to information or actions that a provider has not authorized.
  • The current Metricool-based route does not access LinkedIn private messages. If a future inbox feature is connected, it will require a separate supported provider path and authorization, and this notice will be updated where appropriate before that processing applies.
  • You can withdraw an external-service authorization through that provider's connected-app or permitted-services controls, or contact us for assistance with Axiom-side disconnection.

5. How we use information

  • Provide research, analysis, opportunity qualification, automation and requested deliverables.
  • Respond to inquiries and operate commercial relationships.
  • Maintain continuity across approved Axiom workflows.
  • Measure reliability, diagnose failures, prevent abuse and improve usability.
  • Operate authorized integrations and verify whether external actions actually occurred.
  • Comply with applicable legal, security, accounting and contractual obligations.

6. Service providers

Axiom uses third-party infrastructure and software providers where needed to operate a workflow. Depending on the feature, these may include AppDeploy, Supabase, OpenAI, Composio, Metricool and LinkedIn. Not every provider receives every user's information, and each provider receives only information needed for the function it performs in the relevant workflow.

7. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that operate Axiom; with counterparties when you instruct us to facilitate an introduction or transaction; or where disclosure is required by law, security obligations or the protection of legal rights. Axiom does not sell personal information.

8. Retention

Retention depends on why information was collected, whether a commercial relationship remains active, security and audit needs, and applicable legal, accounting or contractual obligations. We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and then delete, de-identify or isolate it as appropriate.

9. International processing

Axiom operates across markets and uses service providers that may process information in countries different from yours. Where required, we rely on reasonable contractual, technical and organizational safeguards and applicable transfer mechanisms.

10. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, protected service credentials, least-privilege integrations and operational monitoring intended to reduce unauthorized access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), full payment-card details or similar credentials through ordinary inquiry forms or email.

11. Your privacy rights and requests

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have a right to complain to an appropriate supervisory authority. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request so that we do not disclose or alter information for an unauthorized person.

To make a privacy request, email morris@axiomosglobal.com with the subject Privacy Request.

12. Children

Axiom's commercial services are intended for organizations and working professionals and are not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Axiom, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Axiom's services, providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: morris@axiomosglobal.com.
